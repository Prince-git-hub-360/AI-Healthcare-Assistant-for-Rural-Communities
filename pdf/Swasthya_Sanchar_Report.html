💡 Solution Overview

Swasthya Sanchar is an AI-powered healthcare communication platform designed specifically for rural communities. The platform uses Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to extract information from handwritten prescriptions and medical documents. A Large Language Model (Google Gemini) analyzes the extracted medical information, simplifies complex terminology into patient-friendly language, translates the content into the patient's preferred regional language, and generates clear voice guidance. The system also creates medication schedules, sends reminder notifications, maintains digital health records, and supports ASHA workers and caregivers in monitoring treatment adherence. By combining AI, multilingual communication, and voice assistance, the platform ensures that every patient understands their treatment plan regardless of literacy or language barriers.
